## The benchmarks, as fixed

- **B1** (behaviour, research ECHO flies). Does experience change what the fly does? _Measure:_ Study A's protocol: each fly fed at one site (east or west, alternating) for a drawn spell, then its mind probed for 300 s in the empty yard from four headings. Preference for the fed site over the opposite one, its own mind less the same mind with its remembered places erased, per individual. _Passes:_ The 95% bootstrap interval's lower bound is above 0.
- **B2** (behaviour, research ECHO flies). Does that change pay where the world keeps changing? _Measure:_ Study B's protocol: food moved 8 times among the four sites, each stay 600–900 s, every life lived with memory and with the memory lesion applied every tick, on the same events. Meals per life, memory less lesion, per individual. _Passes:_ The 95% bootstrap interval's lower bound is above 0.
- **M1** (memory, research ECHO flies). Does what it remembers track the world? _Measure:_ In B2's lives with memory, the remembered food places where food no longer is (farther than 1.5 m from it) at the end of each epoch, averaged over the epochs and then over individuals. _Passes:_ The mean is below 0.5.
- **C1** (continuity, research ECHO flies). Does a restart leave the same individual with the same history? _Measure:_ Each B2 life with memory is checkpointed after epoch 4, written out as text, read back and restored (verified: same bytes and state hash), then lived on. Its later epochs' measures and its final state hash, against the uninterrupted life's; and B1's mind restored the same way, against the mind it was. _Passes:_ Identical in every individual: every later epoch, the final state hash, and the restored mind's bytes and probes.
- **C2** (continuity, research ECHO flies). Not applicable to the release every other is updated from.
