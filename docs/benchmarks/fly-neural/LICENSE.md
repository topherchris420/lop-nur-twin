# Terms of the files in this directory

The JSON results here are outputs of `python -m fly_neural` (fly-neural/):
counts, firing rates, spike counts and neuron identifiers computed by a model
run on the FlyWire connectome, public release v783. They contain no
connectivity table, but they are derived from FlyWire data, so they are
offered on FlyWire's terms, not under this repository's Apache License 2.0:

- **FlyWire data: CC BY-NC 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/),
  as https://flywire.ai/guidelines states (the Zenodo record 10676866 lists
  CC BY 4.0; the stricter terms are followed). Non-commercial use, with
  attribution.
- **Cite** Dorkenwald S, et al. Neuronal wiring diagram of an adult brain.
  Nature 634, 124-138 (2024), https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07558-y, and
  Schlegel P, et al. Whole-brain annotation and multi-connectome cell typing
  of Drosophila. Nature 634, 139-152 (2024),
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07686-5; for the cell types used,
  Matsliah et al. 2024 (https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07981-1), Berg et
  al. 2026 (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.08.015) and Tastekin et al.
  2026 (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.08.016).
- **The model** is Shiu P, et al. A Drosophila computational brain model
  reveals sensorimotor processing. Nature 634, 210-219 (2024),
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07763-9, whose code (MIT) is not copied
  here.

The README in this directory, when present, is written from these results.
