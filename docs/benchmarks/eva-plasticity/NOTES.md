# Eva embodied plasticity: notes on the records

Written by hand. `dev/` and `heldout/` are generated by
`deploy/garden/service/study.py`; the plan, seeds and decision rule were fixed in
commit `288b3a8` before the held-out run, which ran once from that clean commit.

## Held-out restart: 45 of 48, all three failures in the ablation's harness

The restart check compares each arm's `restart` run with its uninterrupted
`autumn` run. Original, shadow and active matched in all 36 runs. The no-learning
ablation differed in 3 of 12 (seeds 7101, 7108, 7110).

Cause: the ablation holds her learned gains at their prior by resetting them in
memory **after** each `advance`, but `advance` has already saved the world by
then. A restart reloads the saved, learned gains for one step, and that run then
diverges. The defect is in the study's harness for that arm, not in her service
or in the plasticity.

Diagnosis, outside the record: a copy of `study.py` that saves the world after the
reset makes all three restarts identical to their uninterrupted runs (193, 190 and
197 arrivals). `study.py` is left as it ran so the committed records reproduce.
Any later version of the study should persist the reset.

## What the result means

Active mode never swapped a motion in any of the 72 held-out active runs. In every
condition its evidence agreed with her own `choose_motion`: even with weak wings,
flying carried her further per second than walking, and she already chose it. So
active and original are identical, and the primary question's answer is **no
difference established**, with an interval of exactly zero. That is a negative
finding about this mechanism in these conditions, not evidence of harm or benefit.
A secondary contrast, reported without a claim: with her learned gains held at
their prior (no-learning), research gardens in autumn completed fewer journeys
(−90.8, 95% CI −118.5 to −64.6, fewer on 12 of 12 seeds) and took longer over each
(+180 s, CI +109 to +255). Her existing learning is doing work; the addition found
nothing to add on top of it here.
