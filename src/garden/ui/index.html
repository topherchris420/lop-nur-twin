What do these mean?

Each movement is a small experiment in the simulation: it predicts where a movement will take it, tries it, compares the result and saves the comparison. Counts show experience, not a score of intelligence.

Prediction error is measured against the garden's size; lower is closer. Cold, fatigue, wet wings and wear change what a movement achieves, so unfamiliar conditions start uncertain.
